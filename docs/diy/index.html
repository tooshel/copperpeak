DIY & Parts

We like doers. Plenty of AC problems are a cheap part and twenty careful minutes, and there's nothing wrong with saving money by doing it yourself. These guides walk you through the common jobs, we'll sell you the right part, and if a job turns out bigger than it looked, you know who to call.

Know your limits

Every guide is tagged Easy, Moderate, or Advanced. Easy jobs need no tools beyond a screwdriver. Moderate and Advanced jobs mean working inside an outdoor unit that runs on 240 volts, with parts that can hold a dangerous charge even after the power is off. Follow the safety steps every time, or let us handle it.

HARD LIMIT: REFRIGERANT

Anything that involves the refrigerant (recharging, leaks, opening the sealed lines) legally requires an EPA-certified technician. If you hear hissing or see oily spots on the copper lines, that's a call, not a DIY.

We sell parts

Hardware stores carry a few generic parts. We can match the exact part for your system, including the ones they don't stock.
