AC repair, installation & maintenance

In the Valley, air conditioning isn't a comfort upgrade. It's how you get through July. We repair, replace, and maintain every kind of cooling system across the East Valley, and we explain your options before we touch a thing.

AC repair

We diagnose the real problem, show you what we found, and give you up-front pricing before any work starts.

Common signs something's wrong

  • Warm air from the vents, or the house can't get below the mid-80s on a hot afternoon
  • Outdoor unit humming but the fan isn't spinning (often a bad capacitor)
  • Ice on the copper lines or the indoor coil
  • Water in the drain pan, or water stains on the ceiling under an attic unit
  • The system runs constantly and your electric bill jumps
  • Clicking, buzzing, or the breaker tripping
BEFORE YOU CALL

Run through our AC not cooling checklist. A tripped breaker, a clogged filter, or a full drain pan can shut a system down, and you can fix those yourself.

AC replacement & installation

A new system only performs as well as it's installed: refrigerant lines, electrical, drainage, and airflow all have to be done right. We install:

  • Split systems: outdoor condenser with an indoor air handler or furnace
  • Package units: all-in-one rooftop or ground units, very common in Arizona
  • Heat pumps: efficient cooling and heating in one system
  • Ductless mini-splits: for additions, garages, casitas, and hot rooms

In the Southwest, new central AC has to meet a higher federal efficiency standard than the rest of the country (14.3 SEER2 for most home systems), and newer systems use new refrigerants. Our refrigerant FYI explains what that means if you're deciding between repairing an older system and replacing it.

AC maintenance & tune-ups

The best time for an AC tune-up is spring, before the first 100° day. We check and clean what wears out in Arizona heat: capacitors, contactors, condenser coils, drain lines, refrigerant charge, and airflow. A tune-up catches a worn part before it takes something expensive down with it.

We work on all major brands, including Carrier, Bryant, Trane, American Standard, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Amana, York, Payne, Frigidaire, Maytag, Gibson, and many more.

Handy? Do some of it yourself

Cleaning the outdoor coil, changing filters, and flushing the drain line are real money-savers you can do yourself. Start with our DIY guides, and if you need a part, send us a photo of your unit's label and we'll find the exact match.
