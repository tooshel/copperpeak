Cleaner air inside your home

Arizona air brings its own problems: dust storms, pollen most of the year, wildfire smoke, bone-dry winters, and sticky monsoon weeks. Your HVAC system moves all of your home's air, so it's the best place to clean it up.

What affects the air in your home

  • Dust and haboobs: fine desert dust gets past weak filters and leaky ducts, and settles on everything.
  • Pollen: something is blooming in the Valley most of the year.
  • Smoke: from wildfires, cooking, or tobacco.
  • Pets: dander and hair build up in filters and ducts.
  • Chemicals and odors: from cleaning products, paint, new furniture, and carpet.
  • Moisture: monsoon humidity and drain-pan problems can lead to mold and musty smells.

Poor indoor air can show up as allergies, asthma flare-ups, headaches, coughing, sneezing, and dry, irritated sinuses. If those get better when you leave the house, your indoor air is worth a look.

Air quality options we install

  • High-efficiency filtration: media filter cabinets that catch far more than a basic 1-inch filter without choking airflow
  • HEPA filtration: for the finest particles, including smoke and allergens
  • Carbon filters: for odors and chemical fumes
  • UV germicidal lamps: keep the indoor coil and drain pan clean of mold and bacteria
  • Ionizers
  • Whole-home humidifiers: for our dry winters, cracked skin, and static shocks
  • Dehumidifiers: for monsoon season and closed-up rooms
  • Moisture sensors: early warning for drain and leak problems

We'll look at your system and your concerns, then recommend what actually fits. Not every home needs every gadget.

Easy things you can do today

  • Change your filter on schedule. In summer, check it monthly. Here's how to pick and change the right one.
  • Keep the drain line clear so standing water doesn't grow mold: our drain line guide.
  • Run the fan after a dust storm to pull dust through the filter, then check the filter.
  • Vacuum with a HEPA vacuum and wash bedding often in allergy season.
  • Use bathroom and kitchen exhaust fans to vent moisture and cooking fumes.
